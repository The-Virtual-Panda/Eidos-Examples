This is the plan for the channel, in one place: what it is, every video and post, the site, the work of making them, and the sponsors who pay for some of it.

## What is in each folder

- **[The Channel](the-channel)**: four pages: who watches, the kinds of video you make, how you sound on camera, and how the channel makes money. Write these first. Everything else gets checked against them.
- **[Videos](videos)**: one page for each video, Short, live stream, or podcast episode. They sit in a folder for each series, with a folder for each season if the series has them. Add a page as soon as an idea is worth keeping. It grows into the full plan once you decide to make it.
- **[Posts](posts)**: one page for each blog post or newsletter issue, in the same series folders as the videos.
- **[Pages](pages)**: one page for each page of your website: about, sponsors, merch, the newsletter sign-up.
- **[Production](production)**: the to-do list for making things, on a board that runs from Up Next through scripting, filming, editing, and review to Live. One card for each job: the shoot, a thumbnail, booking a guest, checking a fact, sending a sponsor their draft. Each card names the video it is for, and that video's page lists its cards.
- **[Sponsorships](sponsorships)**: one page for each sponsor deal: the brand, the kind of spot, the price, and the terms. A deal stays open until you accept or turn it down. Each video lists the deals it carries.
- **[Files](files)**: thumbnails, scripts, b-roll lists, and sponsor decks. Link them from the pages they belong to. Your logo, fonts, intro, and outro are kept together in the brand kit.

Three pages sit beside the folders. The [Content Calendar](content-calendar.md) says what goes out when. The [Brand Kit](brand-kit.md) says how the channel looks and sounds. The [Rate Card](rate-card.md) is what you send a sponsor who asks what a spot costs.

A video's page says what the video is, not how far along it is. The work lives on the board. A video you drop stays here, marked Shelved, so you remember why.

## Where to start

1. Fill in [Audience](the-channel/audience.md) and [Formats](the-channel/formats.md): who the channel is for, and the kinds of video you make.
2. Add a page in [Videos](videos) for the next video you want to make, starting from its hook.
