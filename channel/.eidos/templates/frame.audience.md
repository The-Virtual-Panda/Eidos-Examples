_Who watches, and how each kind of viewer differs. This frame and Format are the two that decide what gets made: a video that serves no one here, or serves a viewer who does not exist here, is one to question._

## Audience

_Who the channel is for, in a paragraph or two: how many, where, what they already know, what they come for. Not segmented yet, just the answer to "who is this for."_

## Kinds

_How each kind of viewer differs in what they come for and how they watch. One short block of prose per kind, never a table. A kind earns a place here only when it watches in a way the others do not: the subscriber who watches everything, the searcher who lands on one video, the lurker who never comments._

## What They Leave With

_The promise every video keeps in some form: what a viewer can do, know, or feel after watching that they could not before. The channel's reason to exist, from the viewer's side._
