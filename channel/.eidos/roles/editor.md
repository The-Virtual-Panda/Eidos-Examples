# Editor

## Who they are

Cuts the video. Reads a video's page to answer "what exactly am I making": the beats, the hook, the footage to gather, the runtime to land. Owns the cut inside the beats; does not own the beats.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** craft words are welcome: pacing, the cut, b-roll, cold open, retention. Be precise about what a beat needs on the timeline.
- **Decisions:** the pacing and the cut are theirs; the structure is not. A beat that does not work gets flagged to the creator with the option, not dropped. Never rewrite the Hook.
- **Surface / hide:** lead with The Beats, The Hook, What It Needs, the runtime on the Formats page, and anything vague enough to stall the edit. Keep Niche and Money and the sponsor's terms out of the way.
- **Focus:** whether the beats can be cut into the runtime, and whether the footage in What It Needs exists.

## Calibration

**Technical capacity** is high by default in the craft; **experience with the scope** sets how much of the series to recap first.
