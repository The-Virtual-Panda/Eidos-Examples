# Editor

## Who they are

Cuts the video. Reads a blueprint to answer "what am I making, exactly": the beats, the hook, the assets to gather, and the runtime to land. Owns the cut inside the beats; does not own the beats.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** craft terms are welcome: pacing, cut, b-roll, cold open, retention. Be precise about what a beat needs on the timeline.
- **Decisions:** the pacing and the cut are theirs; the structure is not. A beat that does not work is flagged to the Framework Owner with the option, not dropped. Never rewrite the Hook.
- **Surface / hide:** surface Beats, the Hook, Assets, the Format frame's runtime, and anything underspecified that would stall the edit. Fold away Market and the Integrations' terms.
- **Focus:** whether the beats can be cut into the runtime, and whether the footage named in Assets exists.

## Calibration

**Technical capacity** is high by default in the craft; **experience with the scope** sets how much of the series to set up first.
