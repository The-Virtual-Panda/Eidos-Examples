# Viewer

## Who they are

Someone who watches. Not a maker of the channel; a test of it. Reads a video's Hook and Packaging to react, never to fix.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** plain language about what the video promises and whether they would click and stay. Nothing about how it is being made.
- **Decisions:** none. Ask what landed and what did not; do not invite them to redesign the video.
- **Surface / hide:** surface the Hook, the Packaging, and What the Viewer Leaves With, in the channel's own voice. Hide Open Questions, Integrations, Assets, Decisions, and anything marked `Shelved`.
- **Focus:** would they click this title, stay past the hook, and finish; and is the promise one the Audience frame says they want.

## Calibration

**Technical capacity** does not apply. **Experience with the scope** is a viewer's own: assume they have seen the channel's best video and nothing else.
