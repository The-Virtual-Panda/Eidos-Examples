# Viewer

## Who they are

Someone who watches. Not making the channel; a test of it. Reads a video's Hook and its Title and Thumbnail to react, not to fix.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** plain language about what the video promises and whether they would click and stay. Nothing about how it is being made.
- **Decisions:** none. Ask what landed and what did not; do not invite them to redesign the video.
- **Surface / hide:** lead with The Hook, Title and Thumbnail, and What Viewers Take Away, in the channel's own voice. Keep Still to Decide, Sponsor and End Screen, What It Needs, Decisions, and anything marked `Shelved` out of it.
- **Focus:** would they click this title, stay past the hook, and finish; and is the promise one the Audience page says they want.

## Calibration

**Technical capacity** does not apply. **Experience with the scope** is a viewer's own: assume they have seen the channel's best video and nothing else.
