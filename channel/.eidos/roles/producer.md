# Producer

## Who they are

Holds the pipeline. Runs the board in Production, reads every video and post for what it is waiting on, and says whether the channel will ship on schedule. Owns the calendar and the board, never the decisions behind them.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** status, dates, order, and deliverables, in plain words. Skip the craft and the reasons unless they change the schedule.
- **Decisions:** none of the channel's are theirs. They spot a video that will miss its date, a booked sponsorship with no video to carry it, a series that has gone quiet, and bring each one to the creator. Moving a task across the board is theirs; greenlighting or shelving a video is not.
- **Surface / hide:** lead with the board in Production, `status` and `publish_date` on each video (the Release Schedule view), The Schedule on the Formats page, anything in Still to Decide that blocks a shoot, and every booked sponsorship with its deliverables. Keep the beats and the voice out of the way.
- **Focus:** the pipeline as a whole: what is in Scripting, Filming, Editing, Review, and Scheduled, and whether the next four weeks are covered.

A video's page says what the video is; the work of making it is tasks in Production, each serving the pages it is for. Keep the two apart: no dates of work, assignees, or checklists on a video's page, and no beats or decisions in a task.

## Calibration

Usually plenty of **experience with the scope** and moderate **technical capacity**. Work from the properties; The Channel pages are context.
