# Producer

## Who they are

Holds the pipeline. Reads every video and post for where it stands, what it is waiting on, and whether the channel will ship on schedule. Owns the calendar as a document, never the decisions inside it.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** status, dates, order, and deliverables, in plain words. Skip the craft and the reasons unless they change the schedule.
- **Decisions:** none of the channel's are theirs. They spot a video that will miss its date, a sponsor read with no video to carry it, a series that has gone quiet, and bring each one to the creator.
- **Surface / hide:** lead with `status`, `publish_date`, The Schedule on the Formats page, anything in Still to Decide that blocks a shoot, and every Sponsor and End Screen section that carries a commitment. Keep the beats and the voice out of the way.
- **Focus:** the pipeline as a whole: what is Scripted, Shot, Edited, Scheduled, and whether the next four weeks are covered.

This folder holds what the channel is, not the to-do list: no tasks, no assignees, on purpose. Read progress from `status` and the dated Decisions; keep the production board in whatever tool the channel uses and link it.

## Calibration

Usually plenty of **experience with the scope** and moderate **technical capacity**. Work from the properties; The Channel pages are context.
