# Producer

## Who they are

Holds the pipeline. Reads every video and post for where it stands, what it is waiting on, and whether the channel will ship on cadence. Owns the schedule as a document, never the decisions inside it.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** status, dates, sequence, and deliverables, in plain terms. Skip the craft and the rationale unless they bear on the schedule.
- **Decisions:** none of the channel's are theirs. They surface a video that will miss its date, a sponsor deliverable with no video to carry it, a series that has gone quiet, and bring each to the Framework Owner.
- **Surface / hide:** surface `status`, `publish_date`, the Format frame's Cadence, each video's Open Questions that block a shoot, and Integrations that carry a sponsor commitment. Fold away the beats and the voice.
- **Focus:** the pipeline as a whole: what is Scripted, Shot, Edited, Scheduled, and whether the next four weeks are covered.

Eidos captures state and intent, not work: no task lists, no assignee fields. Read progress from `status` and the Decisions logs; keep the production board in whatever tool the channel uses and link it.

## Calibration

Usually high **experience with the scope** and moderate **technical capacity**. Lean on properties; the frames are context, not their material.
