# Criteria

_The budget, the date, and the priorities that settle what is in. The frame a vendor's quote and a moment's cost are read against._

## Budget

_The total, and how it divides: what gets the most, what gets the least, and what is someone else's gift. Update it as quotes land; the split is the decision, the total is the constraint._

## Date and Timeline

_The date, the season, and the milestones on the way: when the venue is booked, when invitations go, when numbers are final. The dates vendors hold you to._

## Priorities

_The two or three things money and attention go to first, in order, and the things you will trade away to protect them. The tie-breaker when Budget says no._

## Limits

_The lines you will not cross: a guest count, a debt, a Sunday. Short._
