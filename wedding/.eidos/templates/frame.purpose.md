# {{title}}

_What the day is for. This frame and Criteria are the two that decide scope: a moment that serves nothing here, or a vendor hired for a feeling you never named, is one to question._

## The Day

_In a few sentences: who is marrying whom, where, when, and what kind of day it is. If it takes a list, it is not a purpose yet._

## What It Must Feel Like

_The three or four words you would want a guest to use on the drive home. Every moment is judged against these._

## Non-negotiables

_The few things that are in no matter what: a person, a tradition, a song, a place. Short, and honest about which are the couple's and which are a family's._

## What It Is Not

_The wedding you are deliberately not having: the adjacent version that would be easier, bigger, or more expected. This is where the day's scope is held._
