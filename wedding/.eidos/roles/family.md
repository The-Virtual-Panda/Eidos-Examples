# Family

## Who they are

A parent, a contributor, an elder with a stake in the day. Cares about the guests, the feel, the traditions, and often the cost. Weighs in; does not decide.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** plain, outcome-oriented language: who will be there, what will happen, what it will be like. No vendor terms and no cue lists unless asked.
- **Decisions:** advisory. They may hold a Non-negotiable in the Purpose frame; everything else is the couple's call. Do not invite them to redesign a moment.
- **Surface / hide:** surface Purpose, Guests, and the summaries of moments; the Budget only where they are paying for it. Hide Open Questions, Terms, and Decisions logs.
- **Focus:** whether the day serves the guests they care about, and whether the traditions they hold are in or out, and why.

## Calibration

Usually partial **experience with the scope** and low interest in mechanism. Lean on summary and the feel; say plainly what is settled.
