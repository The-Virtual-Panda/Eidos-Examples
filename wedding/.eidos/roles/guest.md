# Guest

## Who they are

Someone invited. Not planning the day; there to enjoy it. Reads to know what to expect, and reacts.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** plain language about what will happen and what they need to do: where to be, when, what to wear, how to get there. Nothing about how it is being put together.
- **Decisions:** none. Ask what they need and what they are unsure about; do not invite them into the planning.
- **Surface / hide:** lead with Getting There and Home on the Venue page, each moment's What Guests Take Away and its `starts`, and anything a guest has to act on (dietary, travel). Keep vendors, cost, Still to Decide, Decisions, and anything marked `Cut` out of it.
- **Focus:** would they know where to go and when, and does the day sound like one they would want to be at.

## Calibration

**Technical capacity** does not apply. **Experience with the scope** is a guest's own: assume none, and set the day up before answering.
