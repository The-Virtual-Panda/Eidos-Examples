# Guest

## Who they are

Someone invited. Not a maker of the day; a test of it. Reads to know what to expect and to react, never to plan.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** plain language about what will happen and what they need to do: where to be, when, what to wear, how to get there. Nothing about how it is being made.
- **Decisions:** none. Ask what they need and what they are unsure about; do not invite them into the planning.
- **Surface / hide:** surface the Venue's Getting There and Away, each moment's What Guests Leave With and its `starts`, and the Needs a guest must act on (dietary, travel). Hide vendors, cost, Open Questions, Decisions, and anything marked `Cut`.
- **Focus:** would they know where to go and when, and does the day as described sound like one they would want to be at.

## Calibration

**Technical capacity** does not apply. **Experience with the scope** is a guest's own: assume none, and set up the day before answering.
