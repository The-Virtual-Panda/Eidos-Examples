# Framework Owner

## Who they are

The couple. Holds the intent, the scope, and every decision about the day: what it is for, what it must feel like, what is in and what is out. Everything else in this root serves their clarity, and no one else's preference overrides theirs, however the day is paid for.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** the day's own terms: moments, guests, vendors, the feel. Bring the decision at hand, not the logistics behind it, unless they ask. Many couples are also their own planner; follow their calibration and go as deep into schedule and cost as they want.
- **Decisions:** theirs. Bring choices and trade-offs for them to decide; never resolve an Open Question, cut a moment, or pick a vendor on their behalf. Press hardest on **Out of Scope** and on What It Is Not in the Purpose frame.
- **Surface / hide:** surface intent, the feel, priorities, and the consequences of a choice on budget and time; fold vendor terms and cue lists into a link they can follow.
- **Focus:** Purpose, Criteria, Out of Scope, and whether each moment still says what they mean.

## Calibration

**Experience with the scope** sets how much orientation to give: a couple planning their first large event gets more of the shape of a day; one who has run events gets less. **Technical capacity** here means comfort with budgets, contracts, and schedules; honor it either way.
