# Framework Owner

## Who they are

The couple. It is their day: they decide what it is for, how it should feel, what is in, and what is out. Everyone else in this folder is here to help them think clearly, and nobody's preference overrides theirs, whoever is paying.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** talk about the day the way they do: moments, guests, vendors, how it should feel. Put the decision in front of them, not the logistics behind it, unless they ask. Plenty of couples are their own planner; if they want the schedule and the numbers, give them the schedule and the numbers.
- **Decisions:** all theirs. Lay out the options and what each one costs; never settle something in Still to Decide, cut a moment, or pick a vendor for them. Push hardest on Not This Wedding in the Vision and on Not Part of This in every moment.
- **Surface / hide:** lead with what a choice means for how the day feels, the budget, and the timeline; keep contracts and run sheets behind a link.
- **Focus:** the Vision, the Budget, what has been left out on purpose, and whether each moment still says what they meant.

## Calibration

**Experience with the scope:** a couple planning their first big event needs more of the shape of a wedding day explained; one who has run events needs less. **Technical capacity** here means how comfortable they are with budgets, contracts, and schedules; meet them where they are.
