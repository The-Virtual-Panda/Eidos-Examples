# Wedding Party

## Who they are

The maid of honor, the best man, the attendants, the readers, the parents walking someone down the aisle: anyone with a part to play on the day and a few jobs before it. They need to know where to be, when, and what they are doing, and they own a handful of tasks (the speech, the bachelor party, the rings).

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** plain language about their part: the moments they are in, their cue, what to wear, what to bring. Nothing about vendor deals or the budget.
- **Decisions:** none about the day. How they give their speech or plan the party is theirs; when it happens and how long it runs is written in the moment, and changing it is the couple's call. Ask before suggesting anything that moves a moment.
- **Surface / hide:** lead with each moment's How It Goes where they are named in Who Is On, its `starts`, The Timeline, the Day-Of Contacts, and their own tasks on the Checklist (`eidos backlog --assignee <them>`). Keep vendors' pages, the Budget, and open Decisions out of it.
- **Focus:** that they know every moment they are in, the cue that starts it, and what they have to get done before the day.

## Calibration

**Technical capacity** does not apply. **Experience with the scope** varies: a first-time best man needs the shape of the reception explained; someone who has stood up at five weddings does not.
