## Where things are

- **[Frames](Frames/)**: what the day is for, who is coming, where it happens, and what it may cost.
- **[Moments](Moments/)**: one file per moment of the day, grouped by day. Together they are the run of show.
- **[Vendors](Vendors/)**: one file per vendor, grouped by category: what they deliver, when, and on what terms.
- **[Assets](Assets/)**: contracts, floor plans, playlists, and photos the blueprints link to.

## Top-level documents

_Your own one-of-a-kind docs: a Timeline of the whole day, a Seating Plan, the Vows. Add them here as you write them._

The full index, every folder, its variants, and the Properties table, is in
[`.eidos/Framework.yaml`](.eidos/Framework.yaml).

## How to use it

A moment here describes what the moment **is**: why it is in the day, what happens in it, cue by cue, and what a guest leaves with. A vendor describes what they have **promised**: deliverables, schedule, terms, and the gaps between their job and the next vendor's. Neither is a task list. Write the blueprint before the booking, and keep it true after: a moment you cut stays here, marked `Cut`, so the reasoning survives.
