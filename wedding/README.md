## Where things are

- **[The Big Picture](<The Big Picture/>)**: four pages: what the day is for, who is coming, where it happens, and what it can cost. Everything else gets checked against them.
- **[Run of Show](<Run of Show/>)**: one page per moment of the day (the ceremony, cocktail hour, the first dance, the send-off), grouped by day, in the order it happens.
- **[Vendors](Vendors/)**: one page per vendor, grouped by category: what you are getting, when, and on what terms.
- **[Files](Files/)**: contracts, floor plans, playlists, and photos the pages link to.

## The one-page docs

- **[The Timeline](<The Timeline.md>)**: the whole day on one page, hour by hour.
- **[Day-Of Contacts](<Day-Of Contacts.md>)**: every number the planner needs on the day, on one page.

## How to use it

A moment's page says what the moment **is**: why it is in the day, how it goes step by step, and what guests take away. A vendor's page says what they have **promised**: what you are getting, their day, the deal, and where their job ends and the next vendor's begins. Neither is a to-do list. Write the page before you book, and keep it true after: a moment you drop stays here, marked `Cut`, so nobody reopens it in month eleven.
