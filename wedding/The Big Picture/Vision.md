# Vision

_The big picture. Everything else in this folder gets checked against this page: if a moment or a vendor does not serve what is written here, ask why it is in._

## The Day

_A few sentences: who is getting married, where, when, and what kind of wedding this is. Say it the way you would say it to a friend._

## How It Should Feel

_The three or four words you would want guests using on the drive home. Every moment gets held up against these._

## Must-Haves

_The handful of things that are in no matter what: a person, a tradition, a song, a place. Be honest about which are the couple's and which are a parent's._

## Not This Wedding

_The wedding you are deliberately not having: the bigger one, the more traditional one, the one someone else expects. Writing it down here is what keeps the day from growing._
