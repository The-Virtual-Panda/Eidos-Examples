This is the plan for the wedding, in one place: what the day is for, how it runs, who is helping, and what is left to do.

## What is in each folder

- **[The Big Picture](the-big-picture)**: four pages: what the day is for, who is coming, where it happens, and what it can cost. Write these first. Everything else gets checked against them.
- **[Run of Show](run-of-show)**: one page for each moment of the day, like the vows, the toasts, or the send-off. The pages sit in a folder for each day. On the wedding day itself there is also a folder for each part: getting ready, the ceremony, cocktail hour, and the reception. Add a page when a moment needs a plan of its own.
- **[Vendors](vendors)**: one page for each vendor, sorted by kind: the venue, the caterer, the photographer, the florist. Add a page as soon as you get a quote. Once you book them, it says what they promised.
- **[Checklist](checklist)**: the to-do list, sorted by how far ahead each job is due, from a year out to the thank-you notes. It comes with the jobs most weddings have. Cut the ones you don't need, and add your own.
- **[Files](files)**: contracts, floor plans, menus, playlists, and inspiration boards. Link them from the pages they belong to.

Two pages sit beside the folders. [The Timeline](the-timeline.md) is the whole day hour by hour, to print the night before. [Day-Of Contacts](day-of-contacts.md) has every phone number you'll need on the day.

Each page keeps its own decisions. A decision stays open while it is still a question. Once the couple settles it, it records who decided and when, so nobody reopens it later.

## Where to start

1. Fill in [Vision](the-big-picture/vision.md) and [Budget](the-big-picture/budget.md): what the day should feel like, and what it can cost.
2. Open the [Checklist](checklist) and start on the jobs for a year out: the guest list, the date, and the venue.
