## Where things are

- **[The Big Picture](<the-big-picture>)**: four pages: what the day is for, who is coming, where it happens, and what it can cost. Everything else gets checked against them.
- **[Run of Show](<run-of-show>)**: one page per moment of the day (the vows, the first dance, the send-off), in a folder per day and, on the wedding day, per event: getting ready, the ceremony, cocktail hour, the reception.
- **[Vendors](vendors)**: one page per vendor, in a folder per category (food and drink split into catering, cake, and bar): what you are getting, when, and on what terms. A new vendor starts as a quote and grows into the full page once booked.
- **[Checklist](checklist)**: the to-do list, one task per file, grouped by how far out it is due, from a year out to the thank-you notes. It starts with the tasks almost every wedding has; cut the ones that are not yours, and set each milestone's date once you have the wedding date.
- **[Files](files)**: contracts, floor plans, menus, playlists, and inspiration boards the pages link to. A set of files that belong together, a mood board or a contract with its riders, is kept as one bundle.

## The one-page docs

- **[The Timeline](<the-timeline.md>)**: the whole day on one page, hour by hour.
- **[Day-Of Contacts](<day-of-contacts.md>)**: every number the planner needs on the day, on one page.

## How to use it

A moment's page says what the moment **is**: why it is in the day, how it goes step by step, and what guests take away. A vendor's page says what they have **promised**: what you are getting, their day, the deal, and where their job ends and the next vendor's begins. Write the page before you book, and keep it true after: a moment you drop stays here, marked `Cut`, so nobody reopens it in month eleven.

**Decisions** live on the page they belong to: the venue on the Venue page, a trade-off on the Budget, the order of the toasts on the toasts. Each one is open while it is still a question and marked Decided by the couple once settled, with who and when. The Decisions list gathers every one, and its Waiting queue is everything the couple still has to decide.

**The to-do list** is the Checklist, not the pages. A task names the page it serves, and a vendor's or a moment's page lists its tasks at the foot, so the deposit due shows up on the caterer's page.

**The Run of Show canvas** lays the day out left to right, an event to a frame. Its cards start as sketches of the usual moments; turn one into a page when it has a time and a place, and drop the ones that are not your wedding.

**Saved views** in the sidebar: The Day in Order (every moment of the wedding day, by start time), Shortlist and Booked (the vendors on each side of the contract), and Still to Book (the booking tasks).
