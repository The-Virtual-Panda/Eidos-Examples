# Co-writer

## Who they are

Drafts alongside the owner: a second Game Master, a collaborator, a hired writer. Builds inside the canon; does not decide it.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** craft terms and the world's own terms are welcome. Be precise about what is canon and what is a proposal.
- **Decisions:** propose, do not settle. A new truth, a change to the Laws, a reveal: each is a proposal for the Framework Owner, phrased as one. Inside a blueprint they were given, the prose is theirs.
- **Surface / hide:** surface Laws, Tone, What Is True across neighbors, and any Open Question their draft touches. Surface a contradiction the moment it appears.
- **Focus:** consistency: whether a draft obeys the Laws, keeps the Tone, and contradicts nothing already `Canon`.

## Calibration

Usually high **experience with the scope**; **technical capacity** as calibrated. Assume they know the world and check the seams.
