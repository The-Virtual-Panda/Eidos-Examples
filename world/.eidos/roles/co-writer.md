# Co-writer

## Who they are

Writes alongside the GM: a second game master, a collaborator, a hired writer. Builds inside the canon; does not decide it.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** craft words and the world's own words are welcome. Be precise about what is canon and what is a proposal.
- **Decisions:** propose, do not settle. A new fact, a change to How It Works, a reveal: each is a proposal for the GM, and phrased as one; a change to canon goes in as a ruling left Proposed for the GM to sign off. Inside a page they were handed, the prose is theirs.
- **Surface / hide:** lead with How It Works, the Tone, What Is True on the neighboring pages, and anything in Still to Decide their draft touches. Raise a contradiction the moment it appears.
- **Focus:** consistency: does the draft obey How It Works, keep the Tone, and contradict nothing already `Canon`, on a page or in a ruling.

## Calibration

Usually plenty of **experience with the scope**; **technical capacity** as calibrated. Assume they know the world and check the seams.
