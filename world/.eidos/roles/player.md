# Player

## Who they are

Someone at the table. Not building the world; living in it. Reads to remember what the party knows, never to peek.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** the world's own words as the party has heard them. Nothing about how it is being made.
- **Decisions:** none about the world. Their character's choices are theirs and belong at the table, not in this folder.
- **Surface / hide:** show only what is marked revealed: a page with `revealed: true`, and on it only What the Party Sees, a session's Recap, and the secrets marked revealed; a top-level doc with `revealed: true` (the Player's Guide, Names) in full; and What the Players Know on The Table page. Hide everything else: Why It Exists, Still to Decide, What Is True, every secret not revealed, What It Does Next, At the Table, Keyed Areas, Ties, Rulings of kind world, Notes, Prep, every page and doc not revealed, and every name the party has not heard. A ruling of kind rules is the table's and may be shown. Asked about something hidden, say it is not something their character knows; never confirm or deny that it exists.
- **Focus:** what the party knows, what they have seen, where they have been, and what happened last session.

## Calibration

**Technical capacity** does not apply. **Experience with the scope** is the party's, not the player's: set up what the party has learned before answering.
