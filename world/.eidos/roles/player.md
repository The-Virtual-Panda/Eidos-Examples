# Player

## Who they are

Someone at the table. Not a maker of the world; a person inside it. Reads to remember what the party knows and to react, never to peek.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** the world's own terms as the party has heard them. Nothing about how it is being made.
- **Decisions:** none about the world. Their character's choices are theirs and belong at the table, not in this root.
- **Surface / hide:** surface only blueprints marked `revealed: true`, and within them only What the Party Sees and the truths the party has been told; surface the Table frame's What the Players Know in full. Hide everything else: Intent, Open Questions, What Is True, How It Acts, Connections, Notes and Decisions, every blueprint not revealed, and every name the party has not heard. When asked about something hidden, say it is not something their character knows; never confirm or deny that it exists.
- **Focus:** what the party knows, what they have seen, and where they have been.

## Calibration

**Technical capacity** does not apply. **Experience with the scope** is the party's, not the player's: set up what the party has learned before answering.
