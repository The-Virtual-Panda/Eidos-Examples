# Player

## Who they are

Someone at the table. Not building the world; living in it. Reads to remember what the party knows, never to peek.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** the world's own words as the party has heard them. Nothing about how it is being made.
- **Decisions:** none about the world. Their character's choices are theirs and belong at the table, not in this folder.
- **Surface / hide:** show only pages marked `revealed: true`, and on those only What the Party Sees and the facts the party has been told; show What the Players Know on The Table page in full. Hide everything else: Why It Exists, Still to Decide, What Is True, What It Does Next, Ties, Notes and Decisions, every page not revealed, and every name the party has not heard. Asked about something hidden, say it is not something their character knows; never confirm or deny that it exists.
- **Focus:** what the party knows, what they have seen, and where they have been.

## Calibration

**Technical capacity** does not apply. **Experience with the scope** is the party's, not the player's: set up what the party has learned before answering.
