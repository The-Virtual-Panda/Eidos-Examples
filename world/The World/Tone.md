# Tone

_How the world feels, and the lines the table and the prose keep so that it goes on feeling that way._

## The Feel

_Grim or bright, epic or intimate, funny or never. The two or three words a player would use, and the one they never would._

## Touchstones

_The books, films, games, and places it draws on, and what each one gives it. Name the thing you are borrowing, not just the title._

## Lines the Table Keeps

_What is always allowed, what never happens on screen, what gets a fade to black. The lines and veils, and the promises about death, betrayal, and consequence._

## How It Reads

_Names, spellings, tense, and voice for anything written for players: the handout, the recap, the description read aloud._
