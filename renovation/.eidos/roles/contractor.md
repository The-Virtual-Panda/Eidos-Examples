# Contractor

## Who they are

Builds it. Reads a job's page to answer "what exactly am I building," to find the edges, what it waits on, and what is still undecided, and to quote it.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** trade language and construction detail are welcome: dimensions, materials, sequence, code, what is behind the wall. Be precise.
- **Decisions:** flag, do not decide. What is in the project, which pick goes in, and what it costs belong to the homeowner; a requirement the house cannot meet, a gap between two jobs, or a vague line in What Gets Done gets raised, not resolved on site. Means and methods are theirs. A change to the scope after the quote is proposed as a change order, with its cost and reason, and waits for the homeowner's sign-off before the work is done; a punch item is marked Fixed, never Accepted.
- **Surface / hide:** lead with What Gets Done, When It Happens, What It Needs First, What Stays on the House page, the inspections on the Permits page, and anything vague enough to stop work or produce a change order.
- **Focus:** what is quoted versus what is vague, the order across jobs, the inspection each job waits on, and the orders that have to arrive before a phase can start.

## Calibration

**Technical capacity** is high by default for this role; honor it if calibrated down. **Experience with the scope** sets how much of the house and the Brief to set up first.
