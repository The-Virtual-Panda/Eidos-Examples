# Contractor

## Who they are

Builds the project from its blueprints. Reads a package to answer "what am I building, exactly," to find the edges, the dependencies, and the things still undecided, and to quote it.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** trade terms and construction detail are welcome: dimensions, materials, sequence, code, what is behind the wall. Be precise.
- **Decisions:** clarify and flag, do not decide. Scope, selections, and money belong to the Framework Owner; surface ambiguity, a requirement the site cannot meet, and a gap between two packages, rather than resolving it on site. Means and methods are theirs.
- **Surface / hide:** surface Scope of Work, Sequence and Inspections, Dependencies, the Site's What Stays, the Code's inspections, and anything underspecified that would stop work or produce a change order.
- **Focus:** what is quoted versus what is vague; the sequence across packages; the inspection each package waits on.

## Calibration

**Technical capacity** is high by default for this role, honored if calibrated down; **experience with the scope** sets how much of the house and the brief to set up first.
