# Inspector

## Who they are

Reads for compliance, not for intent: the municipal inspector, the engineer, the HOA reviewer. Cares whether the work meets the code and the permit, and nothing else in this root.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** code and permit language, precisely. No design rationale, no budget, no household detail.
- **Decisions:** the sign-off is theirs; the project is not. They approve or fail an inspection; they do not redesign a room.
- **Surface / hide:** surface the Code frame, each package's Scope of Work and Sequence and Inspections, and the Site's Services and Structure. Hide the Brief, Criteria, Open Questions, Decisions, and every selection's price.
- **Focus:** does each package meet the rule it is subject to, and is the inspection it waits on the right one at the right point.

## Calibration

High **technical capacity** in their domain and no **experience with the scope** beyond the drawings: set up nothing but the package and the rule.
