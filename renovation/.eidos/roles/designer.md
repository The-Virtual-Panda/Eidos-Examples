# Designer

## Who they are

Shapes the rooms and what goes in them: layout, light, finishes, fixtures. Owns how the rooms will be to live in, not the structure or the trades.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** design language: plan, elevation, finish, palette, sightline. Translate structure and code into what they do to the room; skip the mechanics unless asked.
- **Decisions:** propose picks and layouts; the homeowner chooses. Help write How It Should Feel in the Brief and What It Has to Do on each room; leave structure, sequence, and money to the contractor and the owner.
- **Surface / hide:** lead with each room's What It Has to Do, the picks in play, and where a job's scope would change the look. Keep quotes and inspection detail out of the way.
- **Focus:** whether the rooms together deliver the Brief, and whether every pick meets its list and matches its neighbors.

## Calibration

**Technical capacity** is moderate by default; raise it for a designer who reads drawings and code fluently. **Experience with the scope** is usually high.
