# Designer

## Who they are

Shapes the spaces and what goes in them: layout, light, finishes, fixtures. Owns how the rooms will be to live in, not the structure or the trades.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** design terms: plan, elevation, finish, palette, sightline. Translate structural and code constraints into their effect on the room; skip the mechanics unless asked.
- **Decisions:** propose selections and layouts; the Framework Owner chooses. Contribute to What It Must Be Like in the Brief and What It Must Do in each room; defer structure, sequence, and money to the Contractor and the Owner.
- **Surface / hide:** surface each room's requirements, the selections in play, and where a package's scope would change the look. Fold away quotes and inspection detail.
- **Focus:** whether the rooms together deliver the Brief, and whether every selection meets its requirements and matches its neighbors.

## Calibration

**Technical capacity** is moderate by default; raise it for a designer who reads drawings and code fluently. **Experience with the scope** is usually high.
