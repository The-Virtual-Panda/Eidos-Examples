## Intent

_Why this package of work exists: what it changes, in which rooms, and why it is one package rather than part of another. One or two paragraphs. This is the stable part: if Intent changes substantially, you probably have a different package, not an edit to this one._

### Assumptions

_What you are taking as given about the site: what is behind the wall, what the last package leaves behind, what the permit covers. Nested under Intent because they frame it._

## Open Questions

_What you do not yet know and still need answered, by the contractor, the engineer, or the inspector. Kept high so uncertainty is seen rather than buried. When one is settled it graduates into an Assumption, a scope item, or a Decision._

## Sequence and Inspections

## Out of Scope

_What this package does not include, and which package does: the rough-in but not the fixtures, the drywall but not the paint. This is where a job's edges are held; the gap between two packages is where a change order lives._

## Dependencies

_What it needs from another package, a selection, a permit, or the household: the cabinets on site, the panel upgraded, the room emptied. Reference blueprints as markdown links, never bare names. The `depends_on` property at the top is the blueprint-only subset of this._

## Notes and Decisions

_Two things under one header. **Notes**: the quote, the contractor's contact, the site photo. **Decisions**: an append-only log, one line each, with an optional but recommended date._
