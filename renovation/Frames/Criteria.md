# Criteria

_The budget, the schedule, and the priorities that settle what is in. The frame every quote and every selection is read against._

## Budget

_The total, the contingency, and how it divides across phases and rooms. Update it as quotes land; the split is the decision, the total is the constraint._

## Schedule

_The start, the finish that matters (a move-in, a season, a birth), and the milestones between: permits, rough-in inspections, the day the kitchen is unusable._

## Priorities

_The two or three things money and attention go to first, in order, and the things you will trade away to protect them. The tie-breaker when Budget says no._

## Limits

_The lines you will not cross: a number, a date, a wall. Short._
