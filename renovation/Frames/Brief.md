# Brief

_What the owner wants and why. This frame and Criteria are the two that decide scope: a room, a package, or a selection that serves nothing here is one to question, however good the deal._

## The Project

_In a few sentences: what house, what work, and what life it is for afterward. If it takes a list, it is not a brief yet._

## Why Now

_What is wrong, missing, or about to change: the family growing, the roof failing, the sale planned. The problem the work solves._

## What It Must Be Like

_The three or four words you would want to use standing in the finished space. Every room and selection is judged against these._

## What It Is Not

_The project you are deliberately not doing: the addition you talked about, the full gut, the flip. This is where the scope is held._
