## Where things are

- **[The Brief](<The Brief>)**: four pages: what you want, the house as it stands, what it can cost, and what the permits require. Everything else gets checked against them.
- **[Rooms](Rooms)**: one page per room the project touches, grouped by floor: what the finished room has to do.
- **[The Work](<The Work>)**: one page per job, grouped by phase: what gets done, in what order, and what each job waits on.
- **[Selections](Selections)**: one page per fixture, finish, or material, grouped by room: what it has to be, and what you chose.
- **[Files](Files)**: drawings, permits, quotes, spec sheets, and site photos the pages link to.

## The one-page docs

- **[Schedule](Schedule.md)**: the whole project on one page, phase by phase, with dates.
- **[Contacts](Contacts.md)**: everyone on the job, on one page.
- **[Change Log](<Change Log.md>)**: every change to the scope after the quote, dated, with what it cost.

## How to use it

A room's page says what the finished room **is**: why it is in the project, what it has to do, and what it does not get. A job's page says what gets **done** to get there, as a list a contractor can quote and you can walk through afterward. A pick's page says what a thing has to **be** before you buy it. None of them is a to-do list. Write the page before the quote, and keep it true after: a room you drop stays here, marked `Cut`, so the reasoning survives the next conversation about it.
