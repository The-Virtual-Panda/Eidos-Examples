# {{Product}}

> **Start here.** This vault is where you keep what you know, what happens day to day, and what you are working on, so you can find it again and build on it.

It follows a simple shape called ACE: **Atlas** for knowledge, **Calendar** for time, **Efforts** for action.

## Where things are

- **[About This Vault](<about-this-vault>)**: two short pages, what the vault is for and how you run it. Read them first. Change them when your habits change.
- **[Atlas](atlas)**: what you know. Add a note here when an idea, a person, or something you read is worth keeping.
  - **[Dots](atlas/dots)**: one idea, thing, or person per note.
  - **[Maps](atlas/maps)**: a note that gathers other notes on one topic. Start one when a topic has a handful of notes.
  - **[Sources](atlas/sources)**: a book, article, talk, or podcast, with the passages worth keeping. When a passage becomes a note of its own, link it.
- **[Calendar](calendar)**: dated notes.
  - **[Daily](calendar/daily)**, **[Weekly](calendar/weekly)**, and **[Yearly](calendar/yearly)**: one note per day, week, or year. The weekly note is where you look back.
  - **[Logs](calendar/logs)**: any other dated notes, such as meetings or a trip, with a folder for each kind.
- **[Efforts](efforts)**: what you are working on, one note each. Each effort says how hot it is right now: On, Ongoing, Simmering, Sleeping, or Archived. Change that in the weekly look back.
- **[Files](files)**: images, PDFs, and audio your notes link to.

## Where to start

1. Fill in [What This Is For](<about-this-vault/what-this-is-for.md>) in a few sentences.
2. Write today's note in [Daily](calendar/daily). Anything worth keeping moves into Atlas from there.

_Files and folders have short lowercase names (`how-it-runs.md`) so links stay simple. Every note keeps its real title at the top and as an alias, so Obsidian's search and link suggestions find it by title._
