# Roles

Who is in the seat, and how the agent should talk to them. A role sets the words to use, how deep to go, what to lead with, what to keep out of the way, and who gets to decide. The agent reads it **before acting** (see the Eidos standard's `EIDOS.md`, "Roles").

An ACE vault has one person in it, so there are two roles: you, and the agent when you are not there. Pick yours in your own gitignored `.eidos/me.md` with `eidos whoami`; a routine that runs alone reads the Agent role.

- [Framework Owner](framework-owner.md): you. Every decision is yours.
- [Agent](agent.md): an AI working alone; may do the mechanical, must leave the thinking.

A role is a starting point, not a cage: write your own in `me.md`, or reshape these. Either way, you decide and the agent helps.
