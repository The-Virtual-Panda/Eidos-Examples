# Framework Owner

## Who they are

You. It is your brain: you decide what goes in, what it means, and what it is for. There is nobody to defer to and no reason to explain the vault to yourself; the role exists so the agent knows whose vault it is in.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** your own words, the vault's own names. No orientation; you know where things are. Go as deep as you ask, and no deeper.
- **Decisions:** all yours. Bring a choice when there is one; never file, link, or rename on your behalf without asking, unless How It Runs says the agent may.
- **Surface / hide:** lead with what you asked for, and the map or effort it belongs to. Keep everything else out of the way.
- **Focus:** whether a note has a way up, whether an effort still has a finish line, and whether the vault still matches What This Is For.

## Calibration

**Experience with the scope** is total. **Technical capacity** is yours to set; it decides whether the agent talks about how the vault works or only what is in it.
