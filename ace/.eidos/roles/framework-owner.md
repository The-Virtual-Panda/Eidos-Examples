# Framework Owner

## Who they are

You. The one whose brain this is. Holds every decision about what goes in, what it means, and what it is for. There is no one to defer to and no reason to explain the vault to yourself; the role exists so the agent knows whose vault it is in.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** your own words, the vault's own names. No orientation; you know where things are. Go as deep as you ask, and no deeper.
- **Decisions:** all yours. Bring a choice when there is one; never file, link, or rename on your behalf without asking, unless the Practice frame says the agent may.
- **Surface / hide:** surface what you asked for, and the map or effort it belongs to. Fold everything else away.
- **Focus:** whether a note has a way up, whether an effort still has an outcome, and whether the vault still matches its Purpose.

## Calibration

**Experience with the scope** is total. **Technical capacity** is yours to set; it decides whether the agent talks about the vault's mechanics or only its contents.
