## Where things are

- **[Frames](Frames/)**: what the vault is for, and how it runs.
- **[Atlas](Atlas/)**: what you know: one note per idea, source, or thing, and maps that gather them. Flat; a note finds its place by linking `up` to a map, not by its folder.
- **[Calendar](Calendar/)**: when: daily, weekly, and yearly entries, and any other dated series, grouped by series.
- **[Efforts](Efforts/)**: what you are doing: one file per effort, its heat in `status`. The working notes live in Atlas; the effort says what they are for.
- **[Assets](Assets/)**: images, PDFs, and files the notes link to.

## Top-level documents

## How to use it

ACE is Nick Milo's structure for an Obsidian vault: Atlas for knowledge, Calendar for time, Efforts for action. Eidos adds the contract: each folder is a collection with a template per kind of note, each note carries the few properties that make it findable, and two roles say how an agent behaves when you are in the seat and when it is alone. The Atlas template is a title and nothing more, on purpose. An effort's heat is its `status`: On, Ongoing, Simmering, Sleeping, Archived. Move it in the weekly review.
