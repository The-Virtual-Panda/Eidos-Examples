## Where things are

- **[About This Vault](<About This Vault/>)**: two short pages: what the vault is for, and how it runs.
- **[Atlas](Atlas/)**: what you know, in three kinds of note: **Dots** (one idea, thing, statement, or person each), **Maps** that gather them, and **Sources** you read, watched, or heard, each with the highlights worth keeping. The folder says what kind of note it is; a note finds its topic by linking `up` to a map.
- **[Calendar](Calendar/)**: when: Daily, Weekly, and Yearly notes, and every other run of dated notes under Logs, a folder each (Logs/Meetings, Logs/Sermons).
- **[Efforts](Efforts/)**: what you are doing: one note per effort, its heat in `status`. The working notes live in Atlas; the effort says what they are for.
- **[Files](Files/)**: images, PDFs, audio, and files the notes link to; point Obsidian's attachment folder here.

## How to use it

ACE is Nick Milo's shape for an Obsidian vault: Atlas for knowledge, Calendar for time, Efforts for action. Eidos adds the agreement underneath: each folder has a template per kind of note, each note carries the few properties that make it findable, and two roles say how an agent behaves when you are in the seat and when it is alone. A dot's template is a title and nothing more, on purpose. A source's highlights are numbered (H1, H2) and wait as Kept until they become a dot (Linked) or are let go (Dropped), so the highlights still waiting are one list across the vault. An effort's heat is its `status`: On, Ongoing, Simmering, Sleeping, Archived. Change it in the weekly look back.

_Built on Eidos. The page types, the properties each note carries, and the roles live in [`.eidos/`](.eidos/); `eidos check` keeps it honest._
