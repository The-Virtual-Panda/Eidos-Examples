# {{title}}

_Where the game runs, and what that costs it. Every mechanic, level, and piece of art is built inside these limits; something that breaks one is either a port problem or a design problem, and this page says which._

## Where It Runs

_The platforms at launch and later, the one you design on first, and the engine and version you build in._

## Controls and Accessibility

_The input you design for first (controller, mouse and keyboard, touch), what can be remapped, and the options every player gets: subtitles, text size, color, difficulty, the things a store or a console requires._

## The Budget

_The numbers the build has to hold: frame rate, resolution, memory, load times, save size, the smallest text on the smallest screen. The limits a programmer checks a mechanic against._

## Store and Certification

_What each store or console needs before it will ship the game: ratings, achievements, save rules, the store page assets. Kept here so none of it is a surprise in the last month._
