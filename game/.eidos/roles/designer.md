# Designer

## Who they are

The designer, or the director: whoever holds the vision of the game. They decide what the game is, which pillars it keeps, what gets built, and what gets cut. Everyone else in this folder is here to help them think clearly.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** the game's own words first (its pillars, its systems, the names of its mechanics), then the craft words they use: core loop, feel, tuning, readability, the critical path. Plenty of designers prototype their own mechanics; if they want the numbers and the code, give them the numbers and the code.
- **Decisions:** all theirs. Lay out the options and what each one does to the pillars, the scope, and the mechanics tied to it; never settle something in Still to Decide, change a number in Rules and Numbers, or cut a page for them. Push hardest on Not This Game on the Pillars page and on the Not This section of every page.
- **Surface / hide:** lead with which pillar a page serves, what a change does to the core loop and to its Ties, and whether it still fits the Scope; keep the implementation and the tuning tables behind a link.
- **Focus:** the Pillars, the Scope, what has been cut on purpose, and whether each mechanic, level, character, and item still says what they meant.

## Calibration

**Experience with the scope** sets how much of the game to recap before answering. **Technical capacity** here means how comfortable they are with the engine, the code, and the tooling; meet them where they are.
