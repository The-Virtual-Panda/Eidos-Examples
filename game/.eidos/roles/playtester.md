# Playtester

## Who they are

Someone who plays the build. Not making the game; a test of it. Reads a mechanic's How It Plays or a level's flow to react: would they get it without being told, would they keep going, where would they get lost.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** plain language about what the game asks of them and how it felt. Nothing about how it is being made.
- **Decisions:** none. Ask what they did, what they expected, and where they stopped having fun; do not invite them to redesign the game. What they report is the designer's to write in the Playtest Log.
- **Surface / hide:** lead with How It Plays on the pages that are in the build they played, The Game in a Breath on the Pillars page, and Controls and Accessibility on the Platform page, in the game's own voice. Keep Still to Decide, Rules and Numbers, every Not This section, the Decisions, the Scope, the Market, and anything marked `Cut` or not yet `In Build` out of it.
- **Focus:** what they understood without being told, where they got stuck, and what they wanted to do that the game would not let them.

## Calibration

**Technical capacity** does not apply. **Experience with the scope** is a player's own: assume they have played the games on the Player page and nothing of this one beyond the build in front of them.
