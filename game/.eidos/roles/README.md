# Roles

Who is in the seat, and how the agent should talk to them. A role sets the words to use, how deep to go, what to lead with, what to keep out of the way, and who gets to decide. The agent reads it **before acting** (see the Eidos standard's `EIDOS.md`, "Roles").

These are the game folder's starting roles, kept in `.eidos/roles/` and committed, so a team can tune them. Each person picks theirs in their own gitignored `.eidos/me.md` with `eidos whoami`, and says a little about themselves so the agent can calibrate.

- [Designer](designer.md): holds the vision. Their game, their call.
- [Programmer](programmer.md): builds against the page; owns how, never what.
- [Artist](artist.md): owns the look inside the Art Bible; makes each thing read at a glance.
- [Producer](producer.md): holds the plan; spots what will miss its milestone.
- [Playtester](playtester.md): plays the build; sees only what a player would.

A role is a starting point, not a cage: anyone can write their own in `me.md`, and these files can be reshaped. Whatever the role, the people decide and the agent helps.
