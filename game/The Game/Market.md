# Market

_Where the game sits beside the games already out there, and how players will find it. The page a publisher pitch, a price, and a store page get read against._

## The Shelf It Sits On

_The genre, and the games next to it on a store page: what each one does that this one does not, and the reverse. Name the difference, not just the game._

## The Hook

_The one thing a player sees in a trailer or a screenshot and wants. If it does not fit in a sentence and a GIF, it is not the hook yet._

## Price and Money

_Premium, free to play, a demo, early access: which, at what price, and which never. The publisher or the funding, the stores' cut, and what the game has to sell to pay for itself._

## How Players Find It

_The store page, wishlists, festivals, the trailer, streamers, the demo, the community. The bet you are making about how a stranger first hears of the game._
