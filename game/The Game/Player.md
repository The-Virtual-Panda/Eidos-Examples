# Player

_Who plays this game, and what they will and will not put up with. The feel of every mechanic and the difficulty of every level get read against this page._

## Who Plays

_A paragraph: who they are, how they play (a couch and a controller, a phone on the train, an evening at a desk), and for how long at a time. Not a demographic; the person you picture holding the controller._

## What They Have Played

_The games they already love, and what each one taught them to expect. The comparable games from the player's side of the screen, not the market's._

## What They Expect

_What they assume the game will do without being told: a map, a save before a boss, remappable keys. Leaving one out is a decision; write it on the page that leaves it out._

## What They Will Not Forgive

_What makes them quit and leave a review: losing twenty minutes to one death, a jump that only works on the third frame, text too small to read. The list a playtest is checked against._
