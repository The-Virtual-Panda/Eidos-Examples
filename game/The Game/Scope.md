# Scope

_How much game this team can finish, and by when. This page and the Pillars decide what gets built; the Cut List holds what did not make it._

## Team and Time

_Who is making it, how much of their week it gets, and how long you have. The honest version, not the pitch-deck one._

## Size of the Game

_How long it plays and how much is in it: hours, acts, levels, mechanics, characters, items. The ceiling every new idea is weighed against._

## Milestones

_The prototype, the vertical slice, content complete, launch: what each one proves, and roughly when. Not a schedule; the shape of one._

## The Cut Line

_What goes first when time runs short, in order, and what is never cut. Decide it now, while it is calm; a cut made in the last month should already be on this list._
