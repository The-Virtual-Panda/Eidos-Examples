# Playtest Log

_One entry per playtest, newest first: the date, the build, who played, what they did, where they got stuck, and what changed because of it. What testers said goes here in their words. A bug they hit goes on the Backlog, through the **Report a Bug** form or by hand, and lands in Triage. A change it leads to goes in Decisions, naming this entry as its evidence, and on the page it changes._
