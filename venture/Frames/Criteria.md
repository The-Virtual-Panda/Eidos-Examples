# Criteria

_Runway, targets, and the lines that settle what gets pursued. The frame a plan's budget and an investment's size are read against._

## Runway

_Cash in hand, burn, and the month it runs out at the current pace. Updated when it changes; the constraint everything else lives inside._

## Targets

_The numbers the business is trying to hit and by when: revenue, customers, a round closed. Few, and dated._

## Priorities

_The two or three things money and attention go to first, in order, and the things you will trade away to protect them. The tie-breaker when Runway says no._

## Stop Conditions

_What would make you stop: a number missed, a date passed, a belief in the Thesis proved wrong. Written now, while it is cheap to say._
