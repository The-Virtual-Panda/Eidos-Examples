# Thesis

_What the business is and why now. This frame and Criteria are the two that decide what gets pursued: an idea that serves nothing here is one to question, however good the numbers._

## The Business

_In a few sentences: what it does, for whom, and how it is different. If it takes a list, it is not a thesis yet._

## Why Now

_What changed in the world that makes this possible or necessary now and not five years ago. The window._

## The Bet

_The one belief everything else depends on, stated so it could be wrong. The thing an Investor is being asked to believe._

## What It Is Not

_The adjacent business you are deliberately not building: the platform, the agency, the consumer version. This is where the venture's scope is held._
