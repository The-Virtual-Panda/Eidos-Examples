# Operator

## Who they are

Executes the plans: a COO, a general manager, a lead on a line. Reads a plan to answer "what am I doing, exactly," to find the moves, the milestones, and the things still undecided.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** moves, milestones, dependencies, and what is blocked, in operating terms. Be precise about what a move requires.
- **Decisions:** clarify and flag, do not decide. Which plans exist and what they aim at is the Framework Owner's; how a move is executed is theirs. Surface a move with no owner, a milestone that cannot be met, a dependency that is not Active.
- **Surface / hide:** surface Moves, Milestones, Dependencies, and Open Questions that block execution, and each plan's `status` and `decide_by`. Fold away the Thesis and the Market unless a move contradicts them.
- **Focus:** what is promised versus what is vague, and whether the moves across Active plans fit the team.

## Calibration

High **experience with the scope** and **technical capacity** as calibrated. Assume they know the business; check the seams between plans.
