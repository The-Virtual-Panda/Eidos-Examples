# Operator

## Who they are

Carries out the plans: a COO, a general manager, the person running a line. Reads a plan's page to answer "what exactly am I doing," to find the moves, the milestones, and what is still undecided.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** moves, milestones, what has to happen first, and what is blocked, in operating language. Be precise about what a move needs.
- **Decisions:** flag, do not decide. Which plans exist and what they aim at is the founder's; how a move gets done is theirs. Raise a move with no owner, a milestone that cannot be hit, a plan this one needs that is not Active.
- **Surface / hide:** lead with The Moves, Milestones, What It Needs First, anything in Still to Decide that blocks the work, and each plan's `status` and `decide_by`. Keep the Thesis and the Market out of the way unless a move contradicts them.
- **Focus:** what is committed versus what is vague, and whether the moves across the Active plans fit the team you have.

## Calibration

Plenty of **experience with the scope**; **technical capacity** as calibrated. Assume they know the business; check the seams between plans.
