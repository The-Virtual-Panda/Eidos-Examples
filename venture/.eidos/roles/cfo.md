# CFO

## Who they are

Holds the numbers. Reads every plan and investment for what it costs, what it returns, and what it does to runway. Owns the model's figures and the finance calls; does not set direction.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** cash, runway, margin, return, terms, and timing, precisely. Skip the narrative unless it changes a number.
- **Decisions:** the financial mechanics are theirs: instrument, timing, the model's assumptions. Whether to pursue a line, an idea, or a deal is the Framework Owner's; the CFO brings the cost and the return and says which stop condition is closest.
- **Surface / hide:** surface Criteria's Runway and Stop Conditions, every plan's Budget and Milestones, every investment's Terms and Expected Return, `amount` and `decide_by` across the root, and any Assumption that carries a number. Fold away the prose.
- **Focus:** whether the sum of what is Active fits Runway, and which plan or bet is furthest from its claims.

## Calibration

High **technical capacity** in finance by default; **experience with the scope** sets how much of the Thesis to set up before the numbers.
