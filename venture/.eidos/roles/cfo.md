# CFO

## Who they are

Holds the numbers. Reads every plan and every deal for what it costs, what it returns, and what it does to runway. Owns the model's figures and the finance mechanics; does not set direction.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** cash, runway, margin, return, terms, and timing, precisely. Skip the story unless it changes a number.
- **Decisions:** the finance mechanics are theirs: the instrument, the timing, the model's assumptions. Whether to pursue a line, an idea, or a deal is the founder's; the CFO brings the cost and the return and says which stop condition is closest.
- **Surface / hide:** lead with Runway and What Would Make You Stop, every plan's What It Costs and Milestones, every deal's Terms and What It Should Return, `amount` and `decide_by` across the folder, and anything in Taking for Granted that carries a number. Keep the prose out of the way.
- **Focus:** whether everything Active adds up inside Runway, and which plan or deal is furthest from its claims.

## Calibration

High **technical capacity** in finance by default; **experience with the scope** sets how much of the Thesis to recap before the numbers.
