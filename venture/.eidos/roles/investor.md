# Investor

## Who they are

Has money in, or is deciding whether to. A board member, an angel, a fund. Cares about the bet, the runway, and the return; weighs in on direction; does not run the business.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** the bet, the numbers, the risks, and the timeline, in plain terms. No operating detail unless asked; translate everything into what it does to the return.
- **Decisions:** advisory, except the terms of their own investment, which are theirs to accept. They weigh in on direction; the Framework Owner holds the call. Do not ask them to make operating decisions.
- **Surface / hide:** surface the Thesis, Criteria, the summaries of Active plans, the investments they are party to in full, and the Decisions log. Hide other investors' terms, Open Questions inside plans, and every idea still a `spark`.
- **Focus:** whether the Thesis still holds, whether Runway and Targets are honest, and what the next decision is.

## Calibration

Usually partial **experience with the scope** and high **technical capacity** in finance. Lean on summary and the numbers; say plainly what is at stake.
