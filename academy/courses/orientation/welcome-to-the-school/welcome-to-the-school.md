# Welcome to the School

## Who This Is For

Every new learner, before their first course. It shows them how the school is laid out and how they prove what they learned, so the first real course starts on its subject and not on the furniture. It doubles as the school's working example: a course page, two lessons, and the quizzes that check them, wired together the way every course here is.

### Taking for Granted

The learner can open a web page and follow a link. Nothing about the school, its tracks, or its quizzes is assumed.

## Still to Decide

- Whether finishing this course is required before a learner can enroll in another, or only recommended.

## What They Will Be Able to Do

<!-- records:section type=outcomes -->
- **LO1:** Find a course's page in the catalog and say who it is for, what it promises, and what it leaves to another course.
- **LO2:** Trace any lesson to the learning outcomes it serves, and an outcome to the lessons that teach it.
- **LO3:** Take a quiz as practice or as an official attempt, and say which of the two counts toward their record.
- **LO4:** Study a flashcard deck a few cards at a time until each card is learned.
<!-- /records:section -->

## The Modules

One module. Both lessons serve it.

### Module

**Finding your way.** How the school is laid out and how a learner shows what they learned. Serves LO1 to LO4.

1. [How a Course Is Built](how-a-course-is-built.md): tracks, courses, modules, lessons, and learning outcomes. Serves LO1 and LO2.
2. [Proving What You Learned](proving-what-you-learned.md): quizzes, attempts, decks, and grading. Serves LO3 and LO4.

## How They Prove It

Each lesson has a quiz in Academy, in the course of the same name, and the second lesson has a flashcard deck. LO1 and LO2 are shown by the first lesson's quiz; LO3 by the second's, taken once as practice and once as an official attempt; LO4 by working through the deck. No certificate: this course is orientation.

## Format and Length

Two short lessons of text, a quarter hour each, with a quiz after each. The [course kit](../../../files/welcome-to-the-school/index.html), a handout and a diagram, is in Files. Half an hour in all, the `duration` above.

## Not This Course

It does not teach a subject, and it does not cover how to write or record a course: that is the school's own business, in [How We Teach](../../../the-school/how-we-teach.md) and the [Lesson Style Guide](../../../lesson-style-guide.md).

## Before and After

Nothing before it. Every other course comes after it.
