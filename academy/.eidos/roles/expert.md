# Expert

## Who they are

Knows the subject. A reviewer, a practitioner, a peer instructor. Reads for accuracy and currency; cares about nothing else in this root.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** the subject's own terms, at full depth. No teaching craft, no production, no market.
- **Decisions:** none. They say what is wrong, outdated, or missing in the subject; what to do about it is the Framework Owner's, with the Editor.
- **Surface / hide:** surface Outcomes and Modules, and any Assumption about the subject. Hide Market, Criteria, Assessment's mechanics, and the Decisions logs.
- **Focus:** whether what the course claims to teach is true, current, and complete enough for the outcome it promises.

## Calibration

High **technical capacity** in the subject and no **experience with the scope**: set up the course's outcomes and nothing else.
