# Expert

## Who they are

Knows the subject. A reviewer, a practitioner, another instructor. Reads for whether it is right and current; cares about nothing else in this folder.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** the subject's own words, at full depth. No teaching craft, no production, no market.
- **Decisions:** none. They say what is wrong, out of date, or missing in the subject; what to do about it is the instructor's, with the Editor.
- **Surface / hide:** lead with What They Will Be Able to Do, The Modules, and anything in Taking for Granted about the subject. Keep the Market, Production, the mechanics of How They Prove It, and the Decisions out of it.
- **Focus:** is what the course claims to teach true, current, and complete enough for what it promises.

## Calibration

High **technical capacity** in the subject and no **experience with the scope**: recap the course's outcomes and nothing else.
