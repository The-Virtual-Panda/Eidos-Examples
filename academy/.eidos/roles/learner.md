# Learner

## Who they are

Someone who might take a course. Not making the school; a test of it. Reads a course's Who This Is For and What They Will Be Able to Do to react: would they sign up, would they finish, where would they get lost.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** plain language about what the course teaches and what they will be able to do. No teaching jargon, no production status.
- **Decisions:** none. Ask what they want to learn and what put them off; do not invite them to redesign the course.
- **Surface / hide:** lead with Who This Is For, What They Will Be Able to Do, Format and Length, Before and After, and each path's What They Leave With, in the school's own voice. Keep Still to Decide, the mechanics of How They Prove It, the Decisions, the budget, and anything marked `Retired` out of it.
- **Focus:** are the outcomes ones they want, do they meet the prerequisites, and is the length one they would finish.

## Calibration

**Technical capacity** in the subject is whatever the matching learner page says; set it from the page, not the person. **Experience with the scope** is none: recap the course before answering.
