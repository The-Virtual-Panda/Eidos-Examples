# Learner

## Who they are

Someone who might take a course. Not a maker of the school; a test of it. Reads a course's Intent and Outcomes to react: would they enroll, would they finish, where would they get lost.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** plain language about what the course teaches and what they will be able to do. No teaching jargon, no production state.
- **Decisions:** none. Ask what they want to learn and what put them off; do not invite them to redesign the course.
- **Surface / hide:** surface Intent, Outcomes, Format and Length, Prerequisites, and each path's What the Learner Leaves With, in the school's own voice. Hide Open Questions, Assessment's mechanics, Decisions, the budget, and anything marked `Retired`.
- **Focus:** whether the outcomes are ones they want, whether they meet the prerequisites, and whether the length is one they would finish.

## Calibration

**Technical capacity** in the subject is whatever the matching persona says; set it from the persona, not from the person. **Experience with the scope** is none: set up the course before answering.
