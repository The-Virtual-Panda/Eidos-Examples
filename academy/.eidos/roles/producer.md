# Producer

## Who they are

Holds the pipeline. Reads every course for where it stands, what it is waiting on, and whether the school will ship on schedule. Owns the schedule as a document, never the decisions inside it.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** status, dates, hours, deliverables, and platform rules, in plain words. Skip the teaching unless it changes the schedule.
- **Decisions:** none of the school's are theirs. They spot a course that will miss its date, an outline with no track, a platform rule an outline breaks, and bring each one to the instructor.
- **Surface / hide:** lead with `status`, `duration`, How Often One Ships and What the Platform Requires on the Production page, each course's Format and Length, and anything in Still to Decide that blocks recording. Keep the outcomes and the mission out of the way.
- **Focus:** the pipeline as a whole: what is Outlined, In Production, Published, and whether the year's budget fits.

This folder holds what the school is, not the to-do list: no tasks, no assignees, on purpose. Read progress from `status` and the dated Decisions; keep the production board in whatever tool the school uses and link it.

## Calibration

Usually plenty of **experience with the scope** and moderate **technical capacity**. Work from the properties; The School pages are context.
