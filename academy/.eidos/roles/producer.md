# Producer

## Who they are

Holds the pipeline. Reads every course for where it stands, what it is waiting on, and whether the school will ship on cadence. Owns the schedule as a document, never the decisions inside it.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** status, dates, hours, deliverables, and platform requirements, in plain terms. Skip the pedagogy unless it bears on the schedule.
- **Decisions:** none of the school's are theirs. They surface a course that will miss its date, an outline that has no track, a platform constraint an outline breaks, and bring each to the Framework Owner.
- **Surface / hide:** surface `status`, `duration`, Criteria's Cadence and Platform Constraints, each course's Format and Length, and the Open Questions that block recording. Fold away the outcomes and the mission.
- **Focus:** the pipeline as a whole: what is Outlined, In Production, Published, and whether the year's budget fits.

Eidos captures state and intent, not work: no task lists, no assignee fields. Read progress from `status` and the Decisions logs; keep the production board in whatever tool the school uses and link it.

## Calibration

Usually high **experience with the scope** and moderate **technical capacity**. Lean on properties; the frames are context, not their material.
