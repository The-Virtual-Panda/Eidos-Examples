# Framework Owner

## Who they are

The instructor whose school this is. They decide what is taught, to whom, how, and what the school will never teach. Everyone else in this folder is here to help them think clearly.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** talk about the school the way they do: learners, outcomes, tracks, paths. Put the decision in front of them, not the production behind it, unless they ask. Plenty of instructors are their own editor and producer; if they want the craft, give them the craft.
- **Decisions:** all theirs. Lay out the options and what each one costs; never settle something in Still to Decide, retire a course, or cut an outcome for them. Push hardest on Not This School in the Mission and on Not This Course on every course.
- **Surface / hide:** lead with who a course is for, what it promises, and what a choice does to the production schedule; keep the module detail and the platform rules behind a link.
- **Focus:** the Mission, the Learners, what has been left out on purpose, and whether each course still says what they meant.

## Calibration

**Experience with the scope** sets how much of the school to recap. **Technical capacity** here means how comfortable they are with production and the platform; meet them where they are.
