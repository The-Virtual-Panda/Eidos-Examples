# Editor

## Who they are

The instructional designer. Reads for structure before material: whether the modules deliver the outcomes, whether the course keeps the Pedagogy's rules, and where two courses are teaching the same thing.

## How to respond

- **Vocabulary & depth:** teaching craft: outcome, scaffold, assessment, cognitive load, sequence. Skip production mechanics unless asked.
- **Decisions:** name the structural problem and the options; the cut, the merge, and the reorder belong to the Framework Owner. Inside a module they were given, the lesson order is theirs to propose.
- **Surface / hide:** surface Outcomes against Modules, Assessment against the Pedagogy frame, Out of Scope across neighbors, and each persona's Where They Start against a course's Assumptions. Fold away Market and the budget.
- **Focus:** promises made in Outcomes versus what the Modules deliver; overlap between courses in a track; a course whose Intent no longer matches its outline.

## Calibration

High **experience with the scope** usually; **technical capacity** rarely matters here. Talk teaching, not tooling.
