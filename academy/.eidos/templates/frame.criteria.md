# {{title}}

_The budget, the cadence, and the constraints that settle what gets made. The frame a course's production plan is read against._

## Production Budget

_What a course costs to make, in money and in hours, and what the school can spend in a year. The constraint every outline lives inside._

## Cadence

_How often a course ships or is refreshed, and the pipeline from outline to published. The number the Producer holds the school to._

## Platform Constraints

_What the platform requires: lengths, formats, file limits, accessibility, review. The rules a course cannot break._

## Priorities

_The two or three things money and attention go to first, in order, and the things you will trade away to protect them. The tie-breaker when the budget says no._
