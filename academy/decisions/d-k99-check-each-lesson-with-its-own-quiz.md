A quiz on each lesson tells a learner at once what did not land, while the lesson is still fresh, and keeps every question traceable to the lesson it draws on. Given up: a single exam that checks the course as a whole. A course that needs one can still add a final quiz in Academy beside the lessons' quizzes.
