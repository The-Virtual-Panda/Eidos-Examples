## q-nv48

Which kind of attempt counts toward a learner's record?

- [ ] A practice attempt, once it is passed
- [ ] Whichever attempt scored highest
- [x] An official attempt, shared and kept in their history
- [ ] The first attempt of any kind

**Why:** Practice stays on the learner's own machine and can be repeated. An official attempt is shared with the school and kept in their history, and it is what counts.

## q-2ekd

**Type:** written answer

In a few sentences, explain the difference between practice and an official attempt, and why a school might want both.

**Points:** 3

**Model answer:** Practice stays on the learner's own machine and can be repeated as often as they like, so they can learn from each question's explanation without anything being kept. An official attempt is shared with the school and kept in their history, and it is what counts toward their record. Having both lets a learner try freely and then show what they know once they are ready.

**Covers:** Both together: learning freely, then showing it

**Reference:** B-G46#What They Take Away
