## q-enbz

What does a track do?

- [ ] Puts a learner's courses in the order to take them
- [x] Groups the courses that share a subject, in no order
- [ ] Holds the lessons of one course that belong together
- [ ] Lists what a learner can do after a course

**Why:** A track groups courses by subject and puts them in no order. Ordering courses for one kind of learner is a path's job.

**Reference:** B-S53#What They Take Away

## q-6b6b

Which of these is a learning outcome?

- [ ] Covering the history of the subject
- [ ] Watching three videos on composition
- [ ] An introduction to lighting
- [x] Light a portrait with a single lamp

**Why:** A learning outcome is something a learner can do afterward, said so it could be checked. The others name topics or activities, not what a learner can show.

**Reference:** B-S53#What They Take Away

## q-j5rw

**Type:** true or false

Every module of a course serves at least one of its learning outcomes.

- [x] True
- [ ] False

**Why:** Every module serves at least one outcome, and every lesson names the outcomes it serves. An outcome no lesson teaches is a promise the course does not keep.

**Reference:** B-S53#What They Take Away

## q-6hxq

**Type:** match

Match each piece of the school to what it is.

- Lesson → One sitting of a course
- Module → A group of a course's lessons that belong together
- Course → A sequence of lessons toward a set of outcomes
- Path → An ordered sequence of courses for one kind of learner

**Why:** A lesson is one sitting; a module groups lessons; a course strings lessons toward its outcomes; a path strings courses in order for one kind of learner.

**Reference:** B-S53#What They Take Away

## q-hbnd

How is each learning outcome labelled on a course's page?

- [x] LO1, LO2, and on
- [ ] By the module that teaches it
- [ ] By the lesson's title
- [ ] By a letter, A to Z

**Why:** Each outcome is labelled on the course's page as LO1, LO2, and on, so a lesson can link to exactly the one it serves.

**Reference:** B-S53#What They Take Away

## q-kfa9

Why read a course's Not This Course section before enrolling?

- [ ] It lists the course's prerequisites
- [ ] It sets out how the course is graded
- [x] It says what the course leaves out, and which course covers it
- [ ] It names the instructor who teaches it

**Why:** Not This Course says what the course deliberately does not teach and which course does, so a learner does not take it for one it is not.

**Reference:** B-S53#What They Take Away
