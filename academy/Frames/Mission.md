# Mission

_What the school teaches and why. This frame and Criteria are the two that decide what gets made: a course that serves no learner here, or teaches something the mission does not claim, is one to question._

## What We Teach

_In a few sentences: the subject, the level, and the change it makes in a learner. If it takes a list, it is not a mission yet._

## Why It Has to Exist

_What is wrong, missing, or badly taught in the world without this school. The problem the learner has that the existing courses do not solve._

## What a Graduate Can Do

_The promise every path keeps in some form: what a learner can do at the end of the longest path that they could not at the start. Stated so it could be checked._

## What It Is Not

_The adjacent school you are deliberately not running: the bootcamp, the certification mill, the free channel. This is where the scope is held._
