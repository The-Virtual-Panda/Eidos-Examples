Welcome to the School

A one-page handout for the orientation course. Keep it beside you for your first course.

The pieces

Track
Groups the courses that share a subject, in no order.
Course
Lessons toward a set of learning outcomes, labelled LO1, LO2, and on.
Module
The lessons of one course that belong together.
Lesson
One sitting. It names the outcomes it serves.
Path
Courses in order, for one kind of learner.

Proving it

Practice

Official attempt
Shared with the school and kept in your history. This one counts.
Deck
